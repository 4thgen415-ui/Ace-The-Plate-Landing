Frequently asked questions

Does Ace the Plate work offline?

Yes. Your diary, insights, goals, and Poach Lite all work with no connection at all. If you download Poach's optional AI brain once, even the AI coach works fully offline afterwards.

What is Poach, and why is there a separate download?

Poach 🍳 is your in-app coach, cheerleader, guide, and chef. The app ships with Poach Lite built in. For smarter conversations you can optionally download a small on-device AI model (~270 MB, one time, Wi-Fi recommended) from inside the Coach section. We keep it out of the initial app download so the app stays small for everyone. Conversations never leave your device either way.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Ace the Plate to manage, or cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Details are in our Terms.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device, so you're in control: open Me → Reset app data to erase everything instantly, or simply uninstall the app. Nothing lives on our servers. More in our Privacy Policy.

Are the calorie counts exact?

Food database values are careful estimates — real-world foods vary. Ace the Plate is a wellness tool, not a medical device; for medical nutrition guidance, please talk to a physician or registered dietitian.

Can I use kilograms instead of pounds?

Yes — tap the unit toggle on the Weight Trend card (Progress tab) or next to Goal weight in Me. Everything converts instantly.

I found a bug or have a feature idea!

We'd genuinely love to hear it — email support@acetheplate.com with a screenshot if you can. Every report is read by a human.
