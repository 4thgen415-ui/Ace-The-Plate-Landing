# Ace the Plate — Landing Site

> **Achieve, Dine, Progress.** Marketing site and App Store legal pages for
> [Ace the Plate](https://github.com/4thgen415-ui/Ace-The-Plate), the food
> calorie tracker & food log by Appenstance, LLC.

Static, dependency-free HTML/CSS in the Ace the Plate design language
(soft mint/coral/butter glassmorphism, self-hosted Quicksand + Fraunces fonts).

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Landing page (App Store *Marketing URL*) |
| `privacy.html` | Privacy Policy (*Privacy Policy URL*) |
| `terms.html` | Terms & Conditions / EULA with subscription terms |
| `support.html` | Support & FAQ (*Support URL*) |

Deployed to GitHub Pages automatically on every push to `main`
(`.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml`).
