Privacy Policy 🌿

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Ace the Plate is made by Appenstance, LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("we", "us"). We built it privacy-first. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it lives, and the choices you have. The short version: your nutrition data stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter so it can work as a food diary:

  • Profile details you provide (display name, calorie and macro goals, goal weight)
  • Food log entries, custom foods, water intake, and weight check-ins
  • Streaks, points, achievements, and app preferences
  • Conversations with Poach, the in-app coach (kept in memory for your session)

All of this is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit it to our servers — we do not operate servers that receive your personal data. Deleting the app, or using Me → Reset app data, permanently removes it.

2. Poach, the on-device coach

Poach runs entirely on your device. If you choose to download the optional AI model, the download is fetched from a public model-hosting CDN (Hugging Face). That request necessarily exposes your IP address to the CDN, like any file download; it contains none of your diary data. After the one-time download, Poach works fully offline. Your conversations with Poach are never uploaded, logged, or used for training.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of personal data — we don't hold it in the first place
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, or location

4. Camera access

If you use barcode scanning, the camera is used solely to read the barcode in view. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or transmitted.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are purchased through the App Store and processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of your payment information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

6. Health & nutrition data

Nutrition information can be sensitive. Because it is stored only on your device, you are in control of it. We encourage you to use a device passcode. Ace the Plate provides general wellness information only and is not a medical service — see our Terms.

7. Children

Ace the Plate is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data. Because your data is stored on your device, you can exercise these rights directly: view and edit anything in the app, and delete everything via Me → Reset app data or by uninstalling the app. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@acetheplate.com — we respond within 30 days.

9. If we introduce accounts

A future version may offer optional accounts for sync and community features. Before any such feature ships, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected, why, how it is protected, and how to delete your account and data from within the app. We will ask for your consent before moving any existing on-device data to a server.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
